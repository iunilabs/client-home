Modifications, 2026-09-30:
Joined the 11 scan chunks into one mesh and primitive; welded duplicate
vertices; simplified geometry with meshoptimizer; centered the pivot and
normalized the longest dimension to 1 unit; resized the base-color texture
to JPEG (1K for the shared web/mobile version, 2K for the detail alternative);
compressed geometry with Draco.
Original scan normals and material retained. No normal map baked.
